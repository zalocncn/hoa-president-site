This policy covers the HOA President iOS application (the "App") and the website at hoapresidentgame.com (the "Site"). They are different things and are treated separately below.

The short version

HOA President does not collect any data. Not usage data, not device identifiers, not crash reports, not analytics events, not your name, not your location, not anything. There is no account to create and no server to talk to. The app never sends anything anywhere on its own; the only way anything from it leaves your device is if you deliberately share it yourself, using iOS's own share sheet, to somewhere you choose — and even then it goes to that destination, never to us (see §2). This is not a policy choice we could quietly reverse in an update — it's an architectural fact: the app ships with no network layer at all. It contains no networking call, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, and no third-party library of any kind. That absence is enforced by an automated test that fails the build if a forbidden networking symbol is ever introduced.

Everything below exists to make that short version precise and legally complete.

1. What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate a server, a database, an API, or any backend for HOA President. There is no user account, no login, no chat or messaging feature, and no user-generated content that could be transmitted or shared. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or attribution tools of any kind, so we never receive a device identifier, an advertising ID, an IP address, a usage event, a crash log, or any other signal from your device. We do not use Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework because we do not track — there is nothing to ask permission for.

Concretely, this means we have no way to know:

  • That you downloaded or opened the app
  • How you play, how often, or for how long
  • Your device, OS version, region, or IP address
  • Any name you type into the game (see §2)
  • Whether the app crashed

2. What stays on your device — and the one thing you can choose to share

HOA President saves your game locally so you can resume a presidency, keep your best runs, and build up your Archive and Past Presidents gallery. This includes:

  • Your in-progress run and saved game state
  • Your personal bests, unlocked content, and play history within the app
  • Any name you type in for your in-game president (this is fiction — see the app's own disclaimer — and is never validated against, or transmitted to, anything)
  • App settings (sound, haptics, reduce-motion preference)

All of this is written to local storage inside the app's own sandboxed container on your device. The app transmits none of it, because it has no network code capable of transmitting anything — the single, entirely user-initiated exception is the Share button described below. This is local storage under your control, not "collection" by us in any meaningful sense — we never receive a copy, see it, or have access to it.

Sharing your final ledger is entirely your call. At the end of a run, the app offers a Share button and a Copy button for your end-of-term summary — a short line of text (the in-game president name you typed, how many days you lasted, and how it ended) and, optionally, a picture of the final Bulletin. Nothing happens unless you tap it. When you do, the app hands that text and image to iOS's own standard share sheet or your clipboard, and from there it goes only where you send it — a message, a note, a social app, whatever you pick. That destination's own privacy policy then applies to it. The app has no idea where you sent it, and we never receive it, see it, or learn that you shared anything at all, because the app still has no way to talk to us. If you would rather not share the name you typed, don't tap Share.

Deleting the app deletes everything. Because all state lives inside the app's own container and nowhere else, uninstalling HOA President permanently and irreversibly removes all of it. There is no backup copy on our side to delete, because none was ever made — we have nothing to delete on request because we never had anything in the first place.

3. Apple's own data (purchases, device backups)

HOA President is a paid app purchased once through the App Store. That transaction — your Apple ID, payment method, and purchase record — is handled entirely by Apple, which acts as an independent controller of that data under its own privacy policy, not as our processor and not on our instructions. Apple does not share your name, email, or payment details with us, and we do not receive them. If you use iCloud or iTunes/Finder device backups, any backup of the app's local files is governed by Apple's backup and iCloud terms, not by us.

4. No tracking, no advertising, no selling or sharing

We do not track you across apps or websites. We do not serve ads. We do not use any third-party SDK — advertising, analytics, or otherwise — inside HOA President. Because we collect nothing, we have nothing to share, sell, rent, or disclose to any third party, data broker, or advertiser. Stated plainly, as those terms are defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA: we do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done either in the preceding twelve months. We never have, and structurally cannot, since none reaches us. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose beyond those permitted without a right to limit. There are no "service providers," "contractors," or "third parties" processing player data on our behalf, because no player data ever reaches us. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.

Because we do not track, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control browser signal to switch off; we honor them by construction.

5. Children's privacy (COPPA)

HOA President is rated 9+ on the App Store, driven solely by infrequent mild crude humor typical of satire — not by any data practice. Because we collect no personal information from any user, of any age, this app does not collect personal information from children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose children's personal information as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), or the personal data of anyone under 16 as contemplated by the GDPR. There are no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no advertising, and no way for a child — or anyone — to submit information to us through the app. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information to us, email team@delvir.co and we will delete it.

6. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / CPRA and similar laws)

Data protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by the CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete, port, and limit the use of your personal data, to opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. In connection with the app, we have no personal data about you to act on. Because HOA President never transmits, collects, or stores any information about you outside your own device, there is nothing in our possession to access, correct, delete, export, or stop selling.

If you submit a data request to us at the contact address below, we will confirm in writing that we hold no personal data about you, which is the accurate and complete response under both GDPR and CCPA/CPRA given this app's architecture. You may use an authorized agent to make a request; we will still have nothing to disclose. We do not charge for requests and we do not discriminate against anyone who makes one.

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

7. Legal basis for processing (GDPR)

We do not process personal data in connection with the HOA President app, so no lawful basis under Article 6 GDPR is engaged there. For the limited Site and email data described in Sections 10 and 11, our lawful basis is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) in operating and securing our website and in answering messages people choose to send us.

8. International users

HOA President is distributed globally through the App Store. Because no personal data is collected, processed, or stored by us through the app, there is no cross-border data transfer of personal data to describe, and no international data transfer mechanism (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) is required.

9. Security and data breach

The strongest security measure available to a product is not holding the data in the first place, and that is the one HOA President uses: your game exists only on your own device. Because we never receive, store, or process personal data on any server we operate, there is no store of user data that could be breached. In the unlikely event we ever became aware of a security issue affecting the app, we would evaluate our notification obligations at that time, but there is no data flow this policy needs to protect today.

10. This website

The Site is a handful of static files. It sets no cookies of any kind, first- or third-party. It stores nothing in your browser's local storage. It runs no analytics, no advertising or tracking pixels, no session recording, no A/B testing, no embedded social or video widgets, and no third-party fonts or scripts: every image, style, font, and script is served from this domain, and a Content-Security-Policy header on every page instructs your browser to refuse anything loaded from anywhere else. There are no forms, no logins, and no way to submit anything to us through the Site.

What we cannot truthfully claim is that no server is involved at all. The Site is hosted by Vercel Inc., and like every web host, Vercel's infrastructure automatically receives and logs the technical details your browser sends with each request in order to deliver the page, keep the Site available, and defend it against abuse. Those records typically include your IP address, the page or file requested, the date and time, the HTTP status and response size, your browser's user-agent string, and any referring page. Vercel processes this on our behalf as our service provider, under its own security and privacy commitments, and retains it for a limited period on a rolling basis. We do not use these records to identify you, we do not build profiles from them, we do not enrich or combine them with anything else, we do not sell or share them, and we do not connect them to the app in any way — the app never contacts this Site or any other server.
